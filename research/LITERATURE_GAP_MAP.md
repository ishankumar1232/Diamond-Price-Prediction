## 1. Overview & Evaluation Principles

This document maps the **six research contributions** of the DiamondIQ project against the specific limitations and future directions documented across the **10 reviewed research papers**. 

### Status Definitions:
* **Addressed:** The identified gap has been directly formulated, tested with controlled reproducible experiments on actual repository data, and supported by verified empirical metrics.
* **Partially Addressed:** The gap has been experimentally investigated or bounded, but full resolution is constrained by dataset limitations (e.g., absence of live cross-retailer inventory streams or spectroscopic data).
* **Open:** The gap represents an active research challenge requiring external real-world data collection (e.g., continuous wholesale time-series feeds or secondary market resale records) and is honestly documented as outside the scope of static datasets.

## 3. Summary of Project Contributions

1. **Contribution 1 (Data Quality & Integrity):** Audited zero-dimensions (20 rows) and measured their impact on training stability (RMSE improved by \$2.84). Audited 1,308 Length/Width ratio discrepancies in detailed data. Documented software backend input validation as the true mechanism for input rejection.
2. **Contribution 2 (Generalization & Grouped Profile Validation):** Proved identical profile leakage in random splits (117 test rows in holdout, 555 across 5-fold CV) and implemented zero-leakage `GroupShuffleSplit` and 5-fold `GroupKFold`, quantifying the optimistic bias (+10.59 holdout RMSE, +1.75 5-fold CV RMSE).
3. **Contribution 3 (Rigorous Model Benchmarking):** Evaluated 7 distinct regression families under training-only cross-validation with zero test-set data leakage. Reconciled production ($R^2 = 0.9841$, MAE \$257.31 on documented held-out split, disclosing 70 shared input profiles / 0.65%) vs. research benchmark metrics ($R^2 = 0.9832$, MAE \$273.00), strictly excluding the \$231.25 index-shift leakage artifact.
4. **Contribution 4 (Feature Engineering & Interaction Ablation):** Controlled training-only 5-fold CV selection of domain interactions and physical volume. Proved that Carat $\times$ Clarity interaction drives major gains ($-\$26.93$ CV MAE), yielding a test MAE of \$255.96 on untouched test data ($\Delta = -\$33.16$ vs. baseline).
5. **Contribution 5 (Interpretability vs. Causality):** Applied TreeSHAP to quantify global and local feature contributions (base value \$3,926.04), distinguishing game-theoretic Shapley values from heuristic reference comparisons and causal claims.
6. **Contribution 6 (Market Segmentation & Scientific Critique of Origin Classification):** Quantified the 72% to 83% lab-grown market discount across carat brackets, and provided a formal gemological critique showing why superficial 4C-based origin classification reflects scraper missingness and sampling bias rather than physical crystal detection.
